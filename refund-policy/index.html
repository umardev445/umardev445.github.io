General information for monthly class plans. Confirm the current terms before payment.

Monthly plan confirmation

Before paying, confirm the number and length of lessons, billing date, tutor schedule, holidays, make-up lesson rules and cancellation notice period.

Cancellation requests

Send a cancellation request through the listed email or WhatsApp contact before the next billing date. The effective date depends on the current written plan terms.

Missed lessons

Refund or make-up treatment for a missed lesson may depend on who cancelled, how much notice was given and whether replacement time is available.

Contact before payment

For the latest refund and cancellation terms, email umarfarooq360official@gmail.com. This page should be updated with the academy's final confirmed commercial policy before launch.

Keep this page current

Review this policy whenever payment, lesson delivery, data collection or child-safety processes change.

Keep the plan-specific terms in writing

Before payment, confirm the currency, amount, start date, lesson schedule and the refund or cancellation terms attached to that arrangement. Keep the enrollment confirmation and payment record. If a request is needed, include the learner's name, plan, payment date and a concise explanation so the academy can identify the correct enrollment.

Do not rely on a country page or an informal message to infer a different refund condition.
