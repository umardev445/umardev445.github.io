A practical starting point for UK families

QuranCrest offers private online Quran learning with English and Urdu support. Share the learner’s reading level, city, course goal and preferred local lesson windows. Admissions checks the course and tutor match before confirming a recurring appointment.

Choose the course by skill, not school year

A child who can recite a familiar surah may still need help reading Arabic independently. An adult returning after a break may need only selected foundation review. The assessment should distinguish recognition, joining, reading accuracy and memorized recall before a pathway is recommended.

  • Noorani Qaida develops letters, marks and joining.
  • Guided Quran reading develops independent reading from the Mushaf.
  • Tajweed lessons focus on recitation correction and applying rules.
  • Hifz balances new memorization with recent and older revision.

Use the family’s UK clock

The UK uses GMT and British Summer Time at different times of year. A fixed difference from a tutor’s country can therefore become misleading. Send the city and the lesson time you want on your own clock; re-confirm a recurring appointment when clocks change.

Prepare a useful assessment enquiry

For a younger learner, a parent should manage enrollment and know the lesson arrangement. Describe what the child can read without prompting and which material has been used. If you prefer a female tutor, include that request along with the course and timetable; availability is checked for the actual enquiry.

Sample UK enquiry

“We live in Birmingham. My child knows individual letters but needs help joining words. We prefer English explanations and Tuesday or Thursday after school. Please assess the starting point and confirm an available tutor and USD plan.”

Illustrative enquiry, not a student testimonial.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a female Quran teacher?

Yes. Include the preference in your enquiry. The course, language and local-time window are checked before a tutor match is confirmed.

Are monthly fees shown in GBP?

No. Current published plan prices are in USD. Ask about the final payment arrangement and any conversion charges.

Can lessons fit a UK school routine?

You can request after-school or weekend windows. A recurring appointment depends on actual tutor availability and the learner’s needs.
