Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

QuranCrest Academy ("we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of our students, parents, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit https://qurancrest.com or enroll in our online Quran classes.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver live online instruction and manage class scheduling:

  • Contact Information: Parent name, student name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and timezone.
  • Student Information: Age category, current Quran reading level, and preferred tutor specifications.
  • Class Feedback: Progress notes and attendance logs recorded by tutors.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We strictly protect children's online privacy. For students under 18 years of age, all registration, communications, scheduling, and billing must be conducted by a parent or legal guardian. We do not directly collect contact information from minor children without parental consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Schedule free assessments and regular 1-on-1 Quran classes.
  • Match students with suitable male or female tutors.
  • Send progress reports and class updates to parents.
  • Process monthly tuition payments securely.

4. No Data Sharing or Selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal or contact information with third-party marketers. Your data is accessed only by authorized QuranCrest staff and assigned tutors.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to update your stored family information, please email us at info@qurancrest.com.
