Why city and time zone matter

Country names alone are not enough for accurate scheduling. Several countries cover multiple time zones and daylight-saving changes. Share the city and the preferred local lesson window so admissions can check tutor availability correctly.

Do learners receive a different curriculum by country?

The learner's current level and goal determine the course. Location mainly affects scheduling, communication and practical family routine. A beginner in Australia and a beginner in the United States may both need Noorani Qaida, but their suitable lesson windows will differ.

Other countries

Families outside the listed location guides can still request an assessment. Share the country, city, time zone and preferred days. Additional country pages will only be published when QuranCrest can provide genuinely useful local information rather than repeating the same page.
