Is the assessment really free?+

Yes. The 30-minute assessment helps us understand the student’s current level, goals, and suitable schedule. No credit card is required.

Can I request a male or female tutor?+

Yes. Male and female tutor options are available upon request, subject to schedule availability.

Which US time zones do you support?+

We arrange lessons for Eastern, Central, Mountain, and Pacific time zones, including after-school, evening, and weekend options.

Are classes one-to-one or in groups?+

The programs presented on this page are live one-to-one lessons.

Can parents observe the lesson?+

Yes. Parents may observe lessons and can stay involved in scheduling and progress communication.

Do I have to sign a long contract?+

No. Plans are monthly and pay-as-you-go.
