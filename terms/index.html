Assessment and enrollment

The free assessment helps identify a suitable starting point but does not guarantee a particular tutor, schedule or learning outcome. Confirm the current plan details before making payment.

Attendance and scheduling

Students should join on time with the required material and working device. Make-up lesson, rescheduling and holiday arrangements should be confirmed with admissions because availability may vary.

Respectful use

Students, families, tutors and staff are expected to communicate respectfully. Website content may not be copied or presented as another academy's material without permission.

Learning outcomes

Progress depends on attendance, practice, starting level and individual learning pace. QuranCrest does not promise a fixed completion date unless a specific written plan states one.

Keep this page current

Review this policy whenever payment, lesson delivery, data collection or child-safety processes change.

Your enrollment confirmation

Before payment, QuranCrest and the learner or guardian should have a written record of the selected plan, currency, amount, lesson duration, weekly frequency, time zone, recurring time, course, start date and the applicable absence, make-up, cancellation and refund terms. That specific written confirmation should be read together with these website terms.

If a material detail is missing or inconsistent, contact admissions and resolve it in writing before paying.
