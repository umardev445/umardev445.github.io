How tutor matching works

A practical match for the learner, not a random assignment

Tutor preference matters, but a useful match also depends on the learner's present level, course, communication needs and local schedule.

Share the learner's needs

Tell us the learner's age, current level, goal, preferred language, time zone and male or female tutor preference.

Review the starting point

A free assessment helps distinguish between Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed, memorization and Islamic Studies needs.

Confirm a workable match

The coordinator considers teaching fit and availability before discussing a regular one-to-one lesson schedule.

For parents and guardians

Clear boundaries around children's online lessons

A responsible adult should manage enrollment, know the lesson schedule and remain reasonably available for younger children. Learners should not be asked to share payment information, private contact details or sensitive documents during class.

Read child-safety standards
Shared or observable learning area for younger children Parent-managed enrollment and scheduling Respectful, approved communication channels No promotional recording without clear permission
