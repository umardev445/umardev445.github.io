Tutor standards

What we look for in one-to-one teaching

Careful listening

The tutor notices recurring reading patterns and chooses focused corrections instead of overwhelming the learner.

Clear communication

Lessons and family communication can be supported in English or Urdu, depending on tutor availability.

Professional conduct

Respectful online behavior, punctuality and parent-aware expectations are part of the learning environment.
