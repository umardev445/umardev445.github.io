Duas for Entering & Leaving the Toilet: Arabic and Meaning

Learn the dua before entering the toilet and Ghufranaka after leaving, with Arabic, transliteration, meanings and direct hadith references.

Home / Library
By QuranCrest Editorial TeamUpdated 28 October 2026

Quick answer

Before entering the toilet, say the supplication seeking Allah’s protection recorded in Sahih al-Bukhari 142. After leaving, say Ghufranaka, recorded in Sunan Abi Dawud 30. Learn the occasion together with the words so that entering and leaving are not confused.

Before entering the toilet

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْخُبُثِ وَالْخَبَائِثِ

Allahumma inni a‘udhu bika minal-khubuthi wal-khaba’ith.

Meaning: O Allah, I seek Your protection from male and female evil beings. This is an explanatory meaning for learning. The direct reference is Sahih al-Bukhari 142.

After leaving the toilet

غُفْرَانَكَ

Ghufranaka.

Meaning: I ask Your forgiveness. The reference is Sunan Abi Dawud 30. Keeping this short word connected to leaving makes it easier to recall in the correct situation.

A simple learning comparison

Match the words to the occasion
MomentOpening wordWhat you are asking
Before enteringAllahummaProtection
After leavingGhufranakaForgiveness

The cited Hanafi timing answer places the first supplication before entering and the second after coming out. Do not turn a memory aid into a reason to speak the duas inside the toilet.

Teach children without embarrassment

Begin by asking, “Which words belong before entering?” Then practise the short leaving remembrance. A parent can model the words during a lesson away from the toilet and praise correct recall quietly. Avoid making a child explain private bodily matters in a group lesson.

If using a reminder, keep the learning card outside the toilet and treat Arabic text respectfully. Transliteration is temporary help; ask the teacher to hear the sounds in a‘udhu and khaba’ith.

Keep hygiene and remembrance connected

Practical cleanliness, handwashing and respect for privacy remain part of the routine. Reciting a supplication does not replace cleaning. If a child forgets, teach it at the next suitable moment instead of building a frightening story around an ordinary learning mistake.

For questions about a combined shower and toilet room, describe the layout to a qualified teacher or scholar. This page explains these two remembrances, not every bathroom ruling.

Common questions

Which dua is for leaving?

Ghufranaka is the short remembrance recorded for leaving. The longer protection supplication belongs before entering.

Should a child learn both at once?

There is no universal learning schedule. Begin with a manageable part and connect each phrase with its occasion.

Is the English meaning what I recite?

The meaning helps understanding. This page displays the transmitted Arabic separately from transliteration and explanation.
