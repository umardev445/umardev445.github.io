Read Ayatul Kursi, Quran 2:255, with clear Arabic, English meaning, transliteration support, linked recitation and a practical memorization checklist.

Quick answer

Ayatul Kursi is verse 255 of Surah Al-Baqarah, not a separate surah. It teaches Allah’s oneness, perfect life, sustaining power, knowledge and greatness. Read the Arabic below, understand the meaning, and use the linked Quran reference to listen while learning.

Ayatul Kursi in Arabic: Quran 2:255

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ مَنْ ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ

Meaning in English

Allah—none deserves worship except Him, the Ever-Living, the One who sustains all. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him. Everything in the heavens and on earth belongs to Him. Who could intercede with Him except with His permission? He knows what lies before them and what is behind them. They grasp nothing of His knowledge except what He wills. His Kursi encompasses the heavens and the earth, and preserving them does not weary Him. He is the Most High, the Magnificent.

This is an explanatory English rendering prepared for learning. It is not a complete tafsir. For comparison with established translations and word analysis, use the linked Quran and Quranic Arabic Corpus references.

A practical recitation and recall routine

  1. Read one manageable phrase while following the Arabic text.
  2. Listen to the same phrase through the linked verse player.
  3. Repeat carefully and check it with a teacher.
  4. Recall it without looking, then join it to the preceding phrase.
  5. At the next session, review the joined portion before adding more.

Common questions

Is Ayatul Kursi a surah?

It is one verse: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:255. Calling it a separate surah confuses its location in the Quran.

Can I learn only from transliteration?

Transliteration helps at the beginning but does not accurately represent every Arabic sound. Combine the Arabic, reliable listening and teacher correction.
