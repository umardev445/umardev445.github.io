Pricing questions

Know the practical details first

We prefer clarity before enrollment, especially around the learner's timetable and course needs.

Is there a long-term contract?

Published plans are monthly. Confirm any minimum commitment, cancellation notice and refund terms for your selected arrangement with admissions before payment.

Can siblings study on one plan?

Each learner’s course, level and timetable should be discussed individually. Ask admissions to confirm the payment arrangement and whether a sibling option is available before enrolling.

Does the assessment require a card?

No. The free learning assessment does not require a payment card or an obligation to purchase lessons.

Confirm these details before payment

The published plan is a comparison starting point. Your written enrollment confirmation should identify the exact arrangement you are buying.

  • Plan name, currency and total amount
  • Lesson duration and classes per week
  • Learner's city, time zone and confirmed local lesson time
  • Course and tutor match
  • Start date and payment method
  • Holiday, absence, make-up and cancellation rules
  • Refund terms that apply to the confirmed arrangement

Published monthly prices are in US dollars (USD). Confirm the final amount, payment method and available lesson time with admissions before you pay. Your payment provider may apply currency-conversion charges.

Compare a lesson plan with your real routine

Write down available days, lesson length, the learner’s goal and time zone before choosing a plan. Check whether the proposed schedule is realistic during school or work weeks, and confirm the actual arrangement with the team. The learning library can support home practice between lessons.
