Structured Quran Learning Programs

Designed to take students step-by-step from beginner alphabet recognition to fluent recitation and memorization.

Foundation Level

1. Noorani Qaida for Beginners

Build a strong foundation by learning Arabic letters, correct articulation points (Makharij), vowels (Harakat), and basic word joining rules. Perfect for young children starting their Quran journey or adults learning from scratch.

What You Will Learn:
  • Arabic letter shapes & phonetics
  • Fatha, Kasra, & Dammah marks
  • Tanween, Sukoon, & Shaddah
  • Joining compound Arabic words
Recommended For:

Children ages 5+ and absolute adult beginners seeking a patient, step-by-step introduction.

Fluency Level

2. Quran Reading Fluency (Nazra)

Develop confidence and fluency in reading directly from the Mushaf. Tutors guide students through paragraph pacing, verse transitions, and smooth recitation flow.

What You Will Learn:
  • Direct Mushaf reading fluency
  • Stopping & starting rules (Waqf)
  • Building reading stamina & speed
  • Daily Surah recitation practice
Recommended For:

Students who have finished Qaida and want to read full Quran chapters accurately.

Book Free Reading Assessment
Intermediate & Advanced

3. Quran with Tajweed Rules

Learn essential Tajweed rules including Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah, Madd prolongations, Heavy/Light letters, and precise articulation points to beautify recitation.

What You Will Learn:
  • Ghunnah & Iqlab rules
  • Izhar, Idgham, & Ikhfa
  • Madd (stretching) categories
  • Precise Makharij pronunciation
Recommended For:

Intermediate readers wanting to recite Quran in the authentic classical manner.

A structured memorization plan tailored to individual student capacity. Includes daily new lesson recitation (Sabaq), recent revision (Sabaqi), and old revision (Manzil) to ensure retention.
