Course overview

A personal plan built from your current level

Personal lessons that help learners move from hesitant reading toward smoother, more consistent recitation. The tutor listens carefully, demonstrates the next skill and gives focused correction during each live lesson. Progress is guided by readiness rather than pressure.

What the learner can work toward

Improve fluency through guided practice
Correct recurring reading mistakes
Develop a manageable practice routine
Receive feedback at the learner's pace

How lessons are organized

The first assessment identifies the learner's present level and immediate priorities. Regular lessons then combine guided reading, direct feedback and a manageable practice target. Lesson frequency and duration are discussed around age, program and local schedule.
